Product gallery with thumbnails, zoom and lightbox

A shop-style gallery: main picture, thumbnail strip, hover magnifier and a full-screen viewer.

One slider can be a complete product gallery. Mozert builds the thumbnail strip, keeps it in sync, adds hover zoom and opens a full-screen lightbox on click.

new Mozert('.product', {
  thumbs: { position: 'left', count: 4 },  // 'left', 'right', 'top' or 'bottom'
  magnifier: true,                           // button in the corner turns on hover zoom
  lightbox: true,                            // click opens the full-screen viewer
  arrows: true,
  dots: false
});

The thumbnail strip can stand on the left or right of the main picture, and drops below it on small screens. In the lightbox you get next and previous, arrow keys, swipe, click to zoom and Esc to close, with focus handled for keyboard users. Slides with data-video turn into video slides with reel: true, and the lightbox plays them too.

Each thumbnail uses the slide image, or the URL in data-thumb if you want a smaller file. See the Thumbnails, Product gallery and Lightbox examples.
